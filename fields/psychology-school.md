# AI for psychology school

Study areas: EPPP and counseling exams · theory, research methods and case formulation

## What psychology study asks of you

Psychology study asks you to keep theory, evidence and case facts apart. An answer can sound right because it fits a theory while the evidence points elsewhere. Practise naming which part of your answer is theory and which is data. Ace can compare theories from a reading and ask what would weaken each one.

## A study workflow

Distinguish theory from evidence and case facts from assumptions. Use fictional or de-identified teaching material for practice.

## A prompt to try

> Compare the theories in this reading. Ask what evidence supports or weakens each interpretation.

**How do I compare psychological theories?** For each theory, write what it predicts and what evidence would count against it. Ask Ace to test your summary on a fictional case, then check the theories in your assigned readings.

## EPPP AI study tutor

Focus: psychology concept review.

**Workflow.** Define each concept, identify discriminating evidence and compare with the assigned psychology source.

**A prompt to try.**

> Compare the concepts in this psychology lesson. Give me an original question that tests the distinction.

**Where EPPP review usually goes wrong.** Broad review invites recognition without recall: you may recognize a term and still be unable to explain or apply it. Practise defining each concept, then applying it to a short fictional case. Ask Ace to quiz you from your notes and give you a case that tests the line between two similar concepts.

**Is Ace connected to the EPPP?** No. Ace writes original study practice from your material and has no exam-provider affiliation. Confirm content with your program’s resources.

## Counseling theory AI tutor

Focus: theoretical comparison.

**Workflow.** Keep the theoretical assumptions explicit and distinguish them from evidence about the fictional case.

**A prompt to try.**

> Compare two counseling theories from these notes. Ask how each would interpret a fictional course case.

**Where comparing theories usually goes wrong.** Comparisons go wrong when theories are reduced to slogans. For each, state what it says causes the problem, what change looks like and what the counselor does. Apply two theories to the same fictional case and compare the plans. Ask Ace to question your summary of each theory against your readings.

**How do I compare counseling theories?** Use the same fictional case for each theory and compare the explanation and plan each produces. Ask Ace to check your summaries against your assigned readings.
