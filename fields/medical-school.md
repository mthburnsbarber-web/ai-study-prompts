# AI for medical school

Study areas: USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK · physiology, pathology and clinical reasoning

## What medical school study asks of you

Medical school rewards linking a finding to its mechanism: why a lesion causes a sign, why a drug causes a side effect. Lists of buzzwords fall apart when a vignette changes one detail. Use Ace to make you explain the chain out loud, then ask it to change the age, a lab value or the timeline and predict what moves.

## A study workflow

Build a mechanism-first explanation before practising clinical vignettes. Compare your answer with the lecture and an approved reference.

## A prompt to try

> Compare obstructive and restrictive lung disease using this lecture. Ask one question at a time, then explain what I missed.

**How do I stop memorizing buzzwords without understanding them?** Ask for the mechanism behind each association before you accept it, then test it on a changed case. If you cannot say why a finding appears, mark it and return to your lecture or an approved reference.

## USMLE Step 1 AI tutor

Focus: mechanisms and basic science.

**Workflow.** Start with the physiological mechanism, predict its effect and then test it in a new vignette.

**A prompt to try.**

> Explain how a change in preload affects stroke volume. Ask me to predict the direction before explaining the mechanism.

**Where Step 1 practice usually goes wrong.** The common miss is recognizing a pattern without the mechanism behind it, so a vignette that changes one detail breaks the answer. For every association, ask why: which receptor, enzyme or pathway explains it. Ask Ace to change one variable and predict the result, then confirm with your lecture or an approved reference.

**Can Ace give me official Step 1 questions?** No. Ace writes original practice from your own material. It is not affiliated with the USMLE or any question bank, and its explanations should be checked against approved references.

## USMLE Step 2 CK AI tutor

Focus: clinical vignette reasoning.

**Workflow.** Separate the presentation, discriminating finding and teaching conclusion. Use course references to verify the rationale.

**A prompt to try.**

> Give me an original practice vignette on this clerkship topic. Ask for the key finding and my reasoning before showing the teaching answer.

**Where Step 2 CK practice usually goes wrong.** Step 2 CK style questions turn on the next best step, not just the diagnosis. Answers go wrong when the timing, stability or setting in the stem is skipped. Read for those first. Ask Ace to give you a vignette from your notes, commit to a next step and explain why the alternatives come later or not at all.

**How do I practise next-best-step questions?** Decide the diagnosis, then whether the patient is stable, then the single next action, and say all three out loud. Ask Ace to challenge the order and check management against current, approved references.
