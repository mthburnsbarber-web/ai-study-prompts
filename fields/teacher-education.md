# AI for teacher education

Study areas: Praxis and teaching exams · lesson planning, pedagogy and assessment

## What teacher education asks of you

Teacher education asks whether your objective, activity and assessment line up. A lively activity that does not show the objective was met is a common miss. Ace can review a lesson plan on your screen against its objective and ask how you would know, during the lesson, that students understood.

## A study workflow

Align the objective, learning activity and assessment. Revise one step at a time and explain the teaching choice behind each revision.

## A prompt to try

> Review this lesson plan against its learning objective. Ask how I would know whether students understood the concept.

**Can AI review my lesson plan?** Yes, as a second reader. Share the plan on screen, ask Ace to check alignment between objective, activity and assessment, and decide yourself which suggestions fit your students and your program’s requirements.

## Praxis AI study tutor

Focus: teacher preparation review.

**Workflow.** Connect a teaching principle to a concrete classroom example and verify the explanation in course materials.

**A prompt to try.**

## AI for lesson plan study

Focus: learning objectives and assessment.

**Workflow.** Make the objective observable, check the alignment of activities and define evidence of understanding.

**A prompt to try.**

> Review this draft lesson plan. Ask whether the activity and assessment actually measure the stated objective.

**Where lesson plans usually go wrong.** Plans go wrong when the objective names a topic instead of what students will be able to do, which leaves nothing to assess. Write the objective as an observable action, then design the check that shows it. Ask Ace to review a plan on screen and ask how each activity moves students toward that objective.

**What makes a good learning objective?** It says what students will do, under what conditions and how you will know. Ask Ace to test your objective against your assessment, and follow your program’s format.
