# AI for trades and technical school

Study areas: licensing and technical exams · trade theory, diagrams and applied calculations

## What trade school study asks of you

Trade programs combine theory, diagrams and applied calculations with hands-on work that must follow approved procedures. Use AI for the classroom side: vocabulary, reading diagrams and checking calculation steps. Ace can quiz you on a course diagram and ask you to explain the principle behind each component.

## A study workflow

Use course diagrams to understand principles and vocabulary. Follow instructor guidance and approved procedures for practical work.

## A prompt to try

> Quiz me on this course diagram. Ask me to identify components, explain the principle and check the calculation units.

**Can AI help with hands-on trade work?** Use it for study, not for live work. Ace can explain a diagram or check a calculation from your course, but follow your instructor, the manufacturer’s instructions and the applicable code for any practical task.

## Electrical trade school AI tutor

Focus: circuits and technical calculations.

**Workflow.** Identify components and the governing relationship, then verify units and the result using course methods.

**A prompt to try.**

> Walk through this course circuit diagram. Ask for the relationship, assumptions and units before the calculation.

**Where electrical trade study usually goes wrong.** Calculations go wrong on units and on applying a formula to the wrong part of a circuit. Sketch the circuit, label known values with units and say which relationship you are using before calculating. Ask Ace to check each step of your course problems. For practical work, follow your instructor and the applicable code.

**Can AI check my circuit calculations?** It can review your steps on course problems. Verify against your course methods, and never rely on AI for live electrical work; follow your instructor and the applicable code.
