# AI for engineering school

Study areas: FE and PE · problem solving, systems and technical fundamentals

## What engineering study asks of you

Engineering problems fail most often on setup: the wrong system boundary, a missing assumption or units that drift. Draw the system, list assumptions and carry units through every line. Ace can work through a problem one decision at a time with you and ask for a reasonableness check before you accept a result.

## A study workflow

Draw the system boundary, state assumptions and preserve units throughout. Check your result against limiting cases and course methods.

## A prompt to try

> Work through this engineering problem one decision at a time. Ask for assumptions, units and a reasonableness check.

**How do I check an engineering answer?** Check units, test a limiting case and compare the magnitude with what you would expect. Ask Ace to question each assumption, then verify the method against your course notes and references.

## PE exam AI study tutor

Focus: engineering problem reasoning.

**Workflow.** State assumptions and constraints first, then verify the calculated result against a limiting case.

**A prompt to try.**

> Use this engineering course problem to quiz me on the assumptions and constraints before we calculate.

**Where PE practice usually goes wrong.** Problems go wrong on assumptions that are never stated and on applying a method outside its limits. Write down each assumption and check it against the problem. Ask Ace to work through a problem from your discipline one decision at a time and to question any assumption you did not justify.

**Can AI help with PE problems?** It can question your setup and assumptions, but verify methods and values against your discipline’s references and current codes. Do not rely on AI for real design work.
