# AI for architecture school

Study areas: ARE · design critique, building systems and project reasoning

## What architecture school study asks of you

Architecture study combines design judgment with technical requirements. In critique you defend how a scheme meets the brief; in systems courses and exams you show that it works. Practise stating the constraint each choice answers. Ace can look at a concept on screen and question circulation, constraints and trade-offs before your review.

## A study workflow

Explain how a design meets its brief, test constraints and compare alternatives. Verify technical requirements in assigned references.

## A prompt to try

> Challenge this design concept against the brief. Ask about circulation, constraints and the tradeoffs behind my choice.

**Can AI critique my design?** It can ask useful questions. Show the concept on screen with the brief, ask Ace to challenge one aspect at a time and decide which points to address. Verify technical requirements in your assigned references and codes.

## ARE AI study tutor

Focus: architecture exam concepts.

**Workflow.** List the constraints, explain the governing principle and check the relevant course reference.

**A prompt to try.**

## AI for architecture design critique

Focus: design briefs and tradeoffs.

**Workflow.** Tie each design choice to the brief and compare alternatives using explicit criteria.

**A prompt to try.**

> Challenge my design against this brief. Ask how circulation and the stated constraints support my concept.

**Where design critique prep usually goes wrong.** Critiques go wrong when you describe the design instead of defending it. Reviewers ask how a choice serves the brief and what you gave up. Prepare the two or three trade-offs you made and why. Ask Ace to look at your concept on screen and ask the hard questions a reviewer might.

**How do I prepare for a review?** State the brief, your key move and the trade-offs. Ask Ace to challenge each one, rehearse your answers out loud and decide which critiques to address in the next iteration.
