## Optometry optics AI tutor

Focus: geometric optics and sign conventions.

**Workflow.** Draw the optical system, state the convention and track units through the calculation.

**A prompt to try.**

> Work through this optics exercise with me. Ask me to define the sign convention before we calculate.

**Where optics problems usually go wrong.** Most optics errors are sign errors. Light direction, object and image distances and lens power each carry a sign, and switching conventions mid-problem ruins the result. Sketch the ray diagram, write the convention and label every distance. Ask Ace to check one step at a time instead of only the final number.

**What sign convention should I use?** Use the one your course teaches and write it at the top of every problem. Ask Ace to follow that convention explicitly, and check worked examples from your course.
