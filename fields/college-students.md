# AI for college study

Study areas: course exams · lecture notes, coursework and active recall

## What college study asks of you

Most college courses reward active recall over rereading: closing the notes and explaining an idea in your own words. Spaced review then keeps it. Ace can turn a lecture into notes and flashcards and quiz you one question at a time. Use it to practise, and follow your course’s rules on AI for graded work.

## A study workflow

Start with assigned course material, practise explaining it without looking and check your answer against the source.

## A prompt to try

> Use these course notes to quiz me one question at a time. Ask me to explain the idea before giving feedback.

**Is it allowed to use AI for college study?** Policies vary by course and instructor. Practising with your own notes is usually different from submitting AI work as your own. Check your syllabus and ask your instructor when you are unsure.

## AI for college lecture notes

Focus: course notes and active recall.

**Workflow.** Keep the questions tied to the notes and turn missed concepts into a short review list.

**A prompt to try.**

> Turn these lecture notes into five questions. Wait for my answer before showing the explanation.

**Where lecture notes usually go wrong.** Notes go wrong when they copy slides word for word and are never used again. Better notes capture the main ideas in your words and become questions you can answer later. After a lecture, ask Ace to turn your notes into recall questions and quiz you a day later, then again a few days after that.

**Can Ace take notes in my lectures?** Ace can make notes and flashcards from a lecture it hears through your computer’s microphone. Follow your school’s and instructor’s rules on recording, and review the notes against the lecture slides.

## AI for college exam study

Focus: course-based exam preparation.

**Workflow.** Prioritize syllabus topics, practise retrieval and verify explanations against the assigned material.

**A prompt to try.**

> Use my syllabus topics to quiz me one question at a time. Ask me to explain the idea in my own words.

**Where exam study usually goes wrong.** Rereading feels productive but tests poorly. Practise retrieving the material instead: answer questions without notes, then check. Spread sessions over days rather than one long night. Ask Ace to quiz you from your course notes and mix topics, so you practise choosing the right method as well as using it.

**How far ahead should I start studying for an exam?** Earlier and spread out beats one long session. Start with short daily quizzes from your notes and use the last days to review what you miss most often.
