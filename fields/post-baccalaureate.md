# AI for post-baccalaureate study

Study areas: MCAT and DAT preparation · prerequisite sciences and scientific reasoning

## What post-baccalaureate study asks of you

Post-bacc study often packs prerequisite sciences into a short time, then tests them through passages rather than recall. Connect each concept to how it appears in a passage or experiment. Ace can turn a biology or chemistry lesson into a passage-style question and ask you to explain how the evidence supports your choice.

## A study workflow

Connect prerequisite concepts to the passage rather than memorizing isolated facts. Review the reasoning behind each missed question.

## A prompt to try

> Use this biology lesson for a passage-based practice question. Let me explain how the evidence supports my choice.

**How do I prepare for passage-based questions?** Practise reading a short passage, stating its main finding and predicting the answer before looking at the options. Ask Ace for original practice passages from your lessons and review the reasoning on every miss.

## MCAT AI study tutor

Focus: passage-based science reasoning.

**Workflow.** Connect prerequisite concepts to the passage and explain why competing interpretations fit less well.

**A prompt to try.**

> Give me an original passage-style exercise from this biology lesson. Ask which evidence supports my answer.

**Where MCAT practice usually goes wrong.** Passage questions go wrong when outside knowledge overrides what the passage says, or when a figure is read too quickly. Summarize the passage’s main finding and the figure’s axes before answering. Ask Ace for original passages from your science lessons and have it ask which line or data point supports your choice.

**Does Ace provide official MCAT passages?** No. Ace writes original practice and is not affiliated with the AAMC. Use official materials to calibrate format and difficulty.

## DAT AI study tutor

Focus: pre-dental science review.

**Workflow.** Build recall from the assigned science material and check the distinction with a new example.

**A prompt to try.**

> Quiz me on the scientific distinctions in these pre-dental notes. Ask for the reasoning behind each answer.

**Where DAT practice usually goes wrong.** Review goes wrong when sciences are studied only as facts, while the test rewards quick, accurate application. Mix short timed sets with careful review of misses. Ask Ace for original questions from your biology and chemistry notes, and have it ask why each wrong option is wrong.

**Does Ace have official DAT questions?** No. Ace writes original practice and is not affiliated with the test provider. Use official materials to understand the format.
