# AI for divinity and seminary

Study areas: ordination and comprehensive exams · theology, scripture and comparative interpretation

## What divinity and seminary study asks of you

Seminary study asks you to support an interpretation from the text and to represent other traditions fairly. Keep the passage, the interpretation and the tradition distinct. Ace can compare readings from your assigned sources and ask you to support each position with a specific passage.

## A study workflow

Keep textual evidence and interpretation distinct. Compare traditions fairly and rehearse a source-supported argument.

## A prompt to try

> Compare the interpretations in these readings. Ask me to support each position with a passage from the source.

**Can AI explain different theological traditions?** It can summarize and compare, but summaries can flatten real differences. Ask Ace to point to the passage behind each claim, and read the primary and assigned sources yourself.

## Theology AI study tutor

Focus: theological reading and argument.

**Workflow.** Separate the author’s claim, supporting passage and your own interpretation.

**A prompt to try.**

> Compare the arguments in these assigned readings. Ask me to support each claim with a passage.

**Where theological argument usually goes wrong.** Arguments go wrong when a position is attributed to a tradition without the source that supports it, or when an opposing view is summarized unfairly. Cite the text for each claim and state the strongest form of the view you disagree with. Ask Ace to test your argument against your assigned readings.

**Can AI represent theological traditions fairly?** Not always; summaries can flatten real differences. Ask Ace to point to sources for each claim and check them in your assigned readings.

## AI for scripture study

Focus: textual interpretation.

**Workflow.** Read the passage in context, identify the interpretive assumptions and check the assigned commentary.

**A prompt to try.**

> Help me compare interpretations of this assigned passage. Keep the textual evidence distinct from commentary.

**Where scripture study usually goes wrong.** Interpretation goes wrong when a passage is read without its context: the surrounding text, the genre and how your tradition and others have read it. Read the passage in context before interpreting. Ask Ace to compare readings from your assigned sources and ask which words in the passage support each one.

**How should I use AI for scripture study?** Use it to ask questions and compare readings, not as an authority. Read the passage in context and check interpretations in your assigned sources.
