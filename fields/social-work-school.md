# AI for social work school

Study areas: ASWB · practice frameworks, ethics and case analysis

## What social work study asks of you

Social work questions ask you to apply a framework and an ethical standard to a situation, often with more than one reasonable action. Separate the facts given from the assumptions you are adding. Ace can quiz you on a fictional course case and ask which part of the framework or code supports your choice.

## A study workflow

Separate case facts from assumptions. Explain how a framework guides your answer and check the relevant course standard.

## A prompt to try

> Use this fictional course case to quiz me on the practice framework and ethical considerations.

**How do I practise ethics questions?** Use a fictional case, choose an action and name the standard behind it. Ask Ace to present a competing option and explain why yours is better, then check the relevant standard in your course materials.

## ASWB AI study tutor

Focus: social work exam reasoning.

**Workflow.** Identify case facts, relevant ethical considerations and the reason for your answer using approved course sources.

**A prompt to try.**

> Use a fictional case to ask an original social work practice question. Let me explain the ethical considerations first.

**Where ASWB practice usually goes wrong.** Questions often ask what to do first, and answers go wrong by choosing a good action out of order, such as intervening before assessing. Look for the step the situation requires now. Ask Ace for a fictional scenario from your notes and have it question the order of your actions.

**Is Ace affiliated with the ASWB?** No. Ace writes original practice from your material and has no exam-provider affiliation. Check your reasoning against your program’s standards and resources.

## Social work ethics AI tutor

Focus: ethical case analysis.

**Workflow.** Distinguish known facts from assumptions and check the applicable course standards before drawing a conclusion.

**A prompt to try.**

> Help me compare the ethical considerations in this fictional course scenario without inventing facts about the person.

**Where ethics analysis usually goes wrong.** Ethics answers go wrong when a personal value stands in for the professional standard, or when only one obligation is weighed. Name the competing obligations, the standard behind each and how you would resolve the conflict. Ask Ace to present a fictional dilemma and argue the other side.

**How do I practise ethical dilemmas?** Use fictional cases, name each obligation and the standard behind it, then choose and justify. Ask Ace to challenge your choice and check the relevant code in your course.
