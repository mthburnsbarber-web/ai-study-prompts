# AI for business school

Study areas: MBA coursework and GMAT preparation · case analysis, strategy and quantitative reasoning

## What business school study asks of you

Business school cases reward a clear recommendation that survives questioning. Define the decision, show the numbers behind it and name the assumption most likely to be wrong. Ace can play the skeptical classmate: it challenges your recommendation, asks for an alternative and asks which metric would change your mind.

## A study workflow

Define the decision, test assumptions and compare alternatives. Show the calculations behind a recommendation.

## A prompt to try

> Challenge my recommendation for this business case. Ask about an assumption, an alternative and a metric that changes the decision.

**How can AI help with case discussions?** Give Ace your recommendation and supporting numbers, then ask it to argue against you. Answer out loud, revise the weakest assumption and check the calculations yourself before class.

## AI for MBA case analysis

Focus: business cases and recommendations.

**Workflow.** Define the decision and alternatives, test the assumptions and identify a measurable outcome.

**A prompt to try.**

> Challenge my recommendation for this assigned case. Ask about the assumption most likely to change the decision.

**Where case analysis usually goes wrong.** Case write-ups go wrong by summarizing the case instead of deciding. Lead with your recommendation, then the two or three reasons and the numbers behind them, then the main risk. Ask Ace to argue for the strongest alternative so you can test whether your recommendation holds.

**How do I structure a case answer?** Decision first, then reasons, evidence and risks. Ask Ace to challenge each reason, and check your numbers yourself before you submit or present.

## GMAT quantitative AI tutor

Focus: quantitative problem solving.

**Workflow.** State the constraints, solve without skipping assumptions and compare with an independent check.

**A prompt to try.**

> Give me an original quantitative practice problem. Ask for an efficient solution and a second way to check it.

**Where quant practice usually goes wrong.** Quant problems go wrong on setup and on misreading what is asked. Restate the question in your own words, define variables and estimate the size of the answer before solving. Ask Ace for original problems at your level and have it ask how you would check your answer without redoing the work.

**Does Ace provide official GMAT questions?** No. Ace writes original practice and is not affiliated with the GMAT or its owners. Use official materials to calibrate difficulty and format.
