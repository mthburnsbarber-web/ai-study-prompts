# AI for law school

Study areas: bar exam, UBE and MPRE · case briefs, doctrine and issue spotting

## What law school study asks of you

Law school tests whether you can find the rule in an opinion and apply it to facts you have not seen. Reading for the holding, separating dicta and arguing both sides matter more than summaries. Use Ace to question your brief and to build hypotheticals that change one fact, then check every rule against the casebook.

## A study workflow

Brief the assigned opinion before trying a fresh hypothetical. Apply the governing rule to both sides and verify citations against the original authority.

## A prompt to try

> Using this assigned case, separate the facts, issue, holding and reasoning. Give me a new hypothetical that changes one material fact.

**Can AI write my case briefs for me?** It can check yours. Brief the case yourself first, then ask Ace to compare your issue, holding and reasoning with the opinion on screen. Never rely on an AI citation without reading the source; AI can invent or misstate authority.

## AI for law case briefs

Focus: facts, issues, holdings and legal reasoning.

**Workflow.** Read the opinion first, distinguish holding from dicta and check every quotation against the original.

**A prompt to try.**

> Brief this assigned opinion using only the text I provide. Separate the holding from dicta and quote the passage supporting each point.

**Where case briefs usually go wrong.** Briefs go wrong by copying the court’s summary instead of isolating the legally relevant facts, or by stating the holding too broadly. Write the issue as a question the court answered, the holding as its narrow answer and the reasoning as the steps between. Ask Ace to compare your brief with the opinion on screen.

**How long should a case brief be?** Short enough to use in class, often a few lines per part. Ask Ace which facts you could cut without changing the issue. If a fact does not affect the outcome, it may not belong.

## Bar exam AI study tutor

Focus: rule recall and essay issue spotting.

**Workflow.** State the rule from memory, apply facts on both sides and revise the weak part of your answer.

**A prompt to try.**

> Give me an original contracts hypothetical. Let me identify the issues and state the rules before grading my analysis.

**Where bar exam practice usually goes wrong.** Essay answers lose points by missing issues or stating rules vaguely. Recall alone is not enough: you need a rule statement precise enough to apply. Practise writing the rule from memory, then applying it to each fact. Ask Ace for a fact pattern from your outline and have it list the issues you did not raise.

**Can AI grade my bar essays?** It can give feedback on structure and missed issues, but it is not a grader and can misstate law. Compare its comments with your outline, model answers from your program and the governing rules.
