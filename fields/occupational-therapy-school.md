# AI for occupational therapy school

Study areas: NBCOT · occupational performance and activity analysis

## What occupational therapy study asks of you

Occupational therapy keeps three things in view at once: the person, the activity and the environment. Many study answers fail by fixing one and ignoring the others. Practise activity analysis on ordinary tasks, then adapt one element at a time. Ace can ask you to justify each adaptation against the client goal in your course case.

## A study workflow

Keep the person, occupation and environment connected. Compare adaptations and support your reasoning with the assigned framework.

## A prompt to try

> Turn this course case into an activity analysis. Ask me to connect the client goal, task demands and environment.

**What is a good way to practise activity analysis?** Pick an everyday task, break it into steps and demands, then ask Ace to change the client or the setting. Explain how your plan changes and check your reasoning against the framework your program uses.

## NBCOT AI study tutor

Focus: occupational therapy case reasoning.

**Workflow.** Describe the goal, examine the activity demands and justify your answer using the assigned OT framework.

**A prompt to try.**

> Give me an original OT teaching scenario. Ask me to connect the occupational goal with the task demands.

**Where NBCOT practice usually goes wrong.** Case questions go wrong when the answer fixes the impairment and forgets the client’s goal or setting. Read for the occupation the client cares about and the context they live in. Ask Ace for a case from your notes and have it ask how your choice serves that goal, then check your framework.

**Is Ace affiliated with NBCOT?** No. Ace writes original study practice from your material and has no exam-provider affiliation. Use your program’s resources to confirm exam content.

## OT activity analysis AI tutor

Focus: person, occupation and environment.

**Workflow.** Break the activity into observable steps and compare potential adaptations against the stated goal.

**A prompt to try.**

> Help me analyze this fictional daily activity. Ask about the person, task and environment one at a time.

**Where activity analysis usually goes wrong.** Activity analysis goes wrong when it lists steps without the demands each step places on the person. For each step, name the physical, cognitive and environmental demands, then what changes for your client. Ask Ace to change one element, such as the setting, and explain how your adaptation changes.

**What tasks are good for practice?** Ordinary ones: making tea, dressing, using a phone. Break the task into steps and demands, then ask Ace to change the client or setting and question your adaptation.
