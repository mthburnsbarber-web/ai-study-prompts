# AI for accounting school

Study areas: CPA: FAR, AUD, REG and discipline sections · journal entries, audit reasoning and financial reporting

## What accounting study asks of you

Accounting study punishes small slips: the wrong account, a reversed direction or an amount that does not reconcile. Work from the transaction to the entry to the statements, every time, and make the totals balance. Ace can quiz you on an adjusting entry and make you name the accounts and direction before it shows the solution.

## A study workflow

Work from the transaction to the entry and then to the statements. Reconcile the numbers and check the applicable course rule.

> Quiz me on this adjusting-entry problem. Make me identify the accounts, direction and amount before showing the explanation.

## AI for accounting journal entries

Focus: debits, credits and adjustments.

**Workflow.** Identify the economic event, classify the accounts and check that the entry balances.

**A prompt to try.**

> Quiz me on this accounting transaction. Make me explain why each account is debited or credited.

**Where journal entries usually go wrong.** Entries go wrong on direction and timing: a debit and credit swapped, or revenue and expenses recorded in the wrong period. Before writing an entry, say which accounts change, whether each increases or decreases and when. Ask Ace for a transaction, record the entry yourself and have it check each line.

**How do I remember debits and credits?** Learn which side increases each account type, then practise until it is automatic. Ask Ace for transactions and check that every entry balances.
