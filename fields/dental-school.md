# AI for dental school

Study areas: INBDE · oral anatomy, pathology and dental cases

## What dental school study asks of you

Dental school joins detailed anatomy to clinical decisions: a tooth surface, a nerve or a lesion matters because of what it changes in the case. Practise naming the structure, then explaining its clinical consequence. Ace can quiz you from an anatomy chapter on screen and ask how each finding would change your next step.

## A study workflow

Connect anatomy to a case, explain your reasoning and review the source before turning difficult distinctions into flashcards.

## A prompt to try

> Quiz me on the cranial nerves in this dental anatomy chapter. Ask how each finding changes the case.

**How should I study dental anatomy with AI?** Use a labelled diagram from your course, cover the labels and ask Ace to quiz you one structure at a time. After each answer, ask why the structure matters clinically, then confirm against the source diagram.

## INBDE AI study tutor

Focus: integrated dental reasoning.

**Workflow.** Link a foundational concept to a finding, explain the connection and review it in your dental course source.

**A prompt to try.**

> Create an original dental teaching case from this course topic. Ask me to connect the relevant basic science with the findings.

**Where INBDE practice usually goes wrong.** Integrated questions mix basic science into a clinical case, so studying subjects in isolation leaves gaps. When you review a case, name the science underneath each finding: anatomy, pathology or pharmacology. Ask Ace to turn a chapter into an integrated case and to ask which basic science explains each clinical step.

**How do I study for integrated questions?** Take one clinical case and list the basic science behind each decision. Ask Ace to quiz you across those links and check each point against your course sources.

## Dental anatomy AI tutor

Focus: tooth morphology and oral anatomy.

**Workflow.** Sketch or label the structure before answering; use the source diagram to check your distinction.

**A prompt to try.**

> Quiz me on tooth morphology from these notes. Ask me to distinguish two similar features and explain their significance.

**Where dental anatomy study usually goes wrong.** Similar teeth and surfaces blur when you memorize descriptions without comparing them. Study in pairs: two teeth side by side, the features that separate them and how you would tell them apart on a model or image. Ask Ace to quiz you on the distinguishing feature, then check it on your course diagram.

**How do I tell similar teeth apart?** List the features that differ, not the ones they share, and practise identifying each from a diagram. Ask Ace to quiz you on those differences and confirm with your anatomy text.
