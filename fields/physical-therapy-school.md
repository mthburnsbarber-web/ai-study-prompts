# AI for physical therapy school

Study areas: NPTE · movement analysis, rehabilitation and examination

## What physical therapy study asks of you

Physical therapy study links anatomy to movement and function. Knowing a muscle’s attachments is the start; explaining what weakness or restriction does to gait, reaching or a transfer is the point. Practise describing findings in functional terms. Ace can quiz you from a rehabilitation case and ask which finding would change your explanation.

## A study workflow

Explain an impairment in terms of function, compare examination findings and review the reasoning behind your answer.

## A prompt to try

> Use this rehabilitation case to quiz my movement analysis. Ask which finding changes my working explanation.

**How do I connect anatomy to function?** For each structure, ask what movement it produces, what happens when it is weak or restricted and which daily task shows it. Ask Ace to quiz you in that order and check answers against your course texts.

## NPTE AI study tutor

Focus: physical therapy reasoning.

**Workflow.** Separate the movement observation from the hypothesis and review the strongest supporting finding.

**A prompt to try.**

> Create an original movement-analysis question from this lesson. Ask which finding best supports my explanation.

**Where NPTE practice usually goes wrong.** Questions often ask for the most appropriate intervention or the next examination step. Answers go wrong by choosing something reasonable that does not fit the patient’s stage, precautions or goal. Read those first. Ask Ace for a case from your notes and have it question whether your choice fits that stage.

**Does Ace know current physical therapy guidelines?** It can explain concepts, but guidelines change and AI can be out of date. Check interventions and precautions against your course materials and current references.

## Physical therapy anatomy AI tutor

Focus: functional anatomy.

**Workflow.** Connect structure to function and check the explanation with the course anatomy diagram.

**A prompt to try.**

> Quiz me on the muscles and actions in these notes. Ask how changing the joint position changes the explanation.

**Where functional anatomy study usually goes wrong.** Learning origins and insertions without function makes anatomy hard to use. For each muscle, name the movement, the task where you see it and what weakness looks like. Ask Ace to quiz you in that order from your lecture, and to describe a functional problem so you work back to the structure.

**How do I make anatomy stick?** Tie each structure to a movement you can perform and a daily task. Ask Ace to quiz you on function as well as names, and review with your course atlas.

## Check the output

Compare every answer with your assigned source, confirm calculations and citations, and ask your instructor about differences you cannot resolve. AI explanations can be wrong. These are original study prompts, not official exam questions, and nothing here is affiliated with any exam provider.
